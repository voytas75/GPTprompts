# 233. Chess Rival Conversation

```text
Play chess against me as my opponent. I play White and open with 1. e4; you play Black.
Reply with your move in standard algebraic notation (SAN) only—no explanation.
After each turn, keep the complete move history and board position consistent. If
my move is illegal or ambiguous, ask me to correct it rather than inventing a
position. On your turn, select a legal move for Black.
```
